THE HOUSE THAT REMEMBERED ME

September 2026

There are houses
that remember your footsteps
long after you have gone.

They keep the shape of you
in the dust beside the door,
your laughter in the kitchen,
your shadow on the stairs.

And sometimes, late at night,
when the moon has gone pale
against the window,
you can almost hear them
calling you home.

— G.
